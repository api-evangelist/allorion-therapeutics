## About

Allorion pairs medicinal chemistry with internal data and high-throughput screening platforms to
pursue well-validated oncology and immunology targets with mutant-selective and isoform-specific
molecules. The company has raised approximately $100M from investors including Qiming Venture
Partners and INCE Capital, and has partnered its programs with larger developers.

## Pipeline

- **ARTS-011** — a TYK2 inhibitor with selectivity against the other JAK-family kinases; has
  completed a Phase 1 trial in healthy volunteers in China.
- **ARTS-021** — a CDK2 inhibitor shown to selectively inhibit growth of tumors carrying CCNE1
  amplification.
- **ARTS-023 / AVZO-023** — partnered with Avenzo Therapeutics and in Phase 1 evaluation in the
  United States for advanced solid tumors.
- **Allosteric EGFR L858R inhibitor** — subject of an exclusive option and global license agreement
  with AstraZeneca, aimed at advanced EGFR-mutant non-small-cell lung cancer.
